# League Tracker
 
A simple, easy way to record how you're doing match to match on League of Legends.

## Who the app is for

Anyone who plays ranked or casual matches in a role/character-based game (like League of Legends) and wants a quick, personal record of how each game went, without having to dig through an in-game match history or a spreadsheet you have to meticulously manage.

## What it does

League Tracker lets you record a match right after you finish it: the **role** you played, the **champion** you picked, your **KDA & CS**, the **length of the game**, and whether you **won or lost**. Every match gets added to a running list that you can sort by **role**, **champion**, or **result**.

## Why it's useful
 
In-game stats disappear or get buried fast once you've played a lot of matches, and most games don't let you sort or filter your own history. League Tracker keeps a lightweight, personal record so you can look back and spot trends: like which roles or champions you're winning on, or where you might want to make a change.

## Features
 
- **Log a match** — enter role, champion, KDA & CS, length of game, and win/loss for each match
- **Match history list** — every logged match shows up in a running list
- **Sort by role** — group your matches by the role you played
- **Sort by champion** - group your matches by the champion you played
- **Sort by result** — see wins or losses grouped exclusively
- **Clear sort** — reset back to the order you logged matches in
