# League Tracker
 
A simple, easy way to record how you're doing match to match on League of Legends.

## Who the app is for

Anyone who plays ranked or casual matches in a role/character-based game (like League of Legends) and wants a quick, personal record of how each game went, without having to dig through an in-game match history or a spreadsheet you have to meticulously manage.

## What it does

League Tracker lets you record a match right after you finish it: the **role** you played, the **champion** you picked, your **KDA & CS**, the **time of the game**, and whether you **won or lost**. Every match gets added to a running list that you can sorted by **role**, **champion**, or **result**.

## Why it's useful
 
In game stats disappear or get buried fast by playing many games, also most games don't sort your games for you. League Tracker keeps a lightweight, personal record so you can look back and spot trends: like which roles or champions you're winning on, or whether you should change up on something.
